# CypherBuddy Privacy Policy & Data Usage Policy

## 1. Data Minimization & Principles
CypherBuddy is built around data minimization. We collect only what is strictly necessary to perform threat scanning and deliver visual digital troubleshooting assistance.

- **No Chat Log Ingestion**: CypherBuddy never reads, monitors, or stores your private personal messages or chats.
- **Explicit Consent**: Parent alert sharing and device connections require explicit mutual consent from all involved family members.
- **Zero Plaintext Passwords**: User passwords are stored using salted Bcrypt hashes.

---

## 2. Information Handled & Purpose

| Category | Processing Purpose | Retention Policy |
|---|---|---|
| **Account Data** (Email, Name) | Authentication & Role Authorization | Stored until user requests account deletion |
| **Scan Targets** (URLs, Hashes) | Malware & Phishing Risk Evaluation | Retained in audit history for PDF export |
| **Quarantined Files** | Isolated SHA-256 analysis | Automatically purged from quarantine after 24h |

---

## 3. Account Deletion & Rights
Users can delete their account history and reports at any time by navigating to **Settings -> Privacy -> Delete Account**.
